## F. Dataroom checklist

1. **Historical Sarafu Network evidence**
   - Archive dated swap volume, active-user and asset definitions, incidents, redemption presentments, aging vouchers, confirmed fulfillment, discharge, holding-duration measures, periods, exclusions, and methodology separately from current Cosmo-Local Credit metrics.
   - Preserve the [historical Dune Analytics dashboard](https://dune.com/grassrootseconomics/sarafu-network).

2. **Proposed fee-enforcement evidence, if implemented**
   - Show that any fee hook and registry gating are integrated into the executed transaction path rather than enforced only by an interface.
   - Provide tests showing that the applicable execution service rejects a hop whose receipt does not prove fee collection under the adopted policy.
   - Publish test vectors, failure cases, eligible assets, and compliant Pools tied to an identified registry version.
   - Link the applicable [software description](https://software.grassecon.org).

3. **Guarantor and coverage pack**
   - Publish eligibility, responsible parties, funding, caps, premiums where applicable, triggers, evidence, exclusions, decision authority, and dispute and appeal examples.
   - Include sample voucher and Pool disclosures that distinguish issuer responsibility from any expressly offered Pool protection or third-party guarantee.
   - Preserve the [historical Sarafu Network reports archive](https://sarafu.network/reports) and [historical year-in-review presentation](https://youtu.be/5yGaP31bvs0?si=fZ-AMTEXsmVR8Ulv) as lineage evidence, not proof of current CLC coverage or adoption.

4. **Legal and compliance pack**
   - Document jurisdiction strategy, voucher classes, cash-equivalent policies, KYC or attestation options, geofencing, consumer disclosures, mis-selling controls, and the distinction between an ordinary Pool swap and an express credit product.
   - Link the effective [Cosmo-Local Credit Terms of Service](/governance/terms) and preserve superseded versions through controlled records.

5. **Governance hardening**
   - Provide conflict-of-interest and recusal procedures, decision and appeal records, sanctions, registry-removal due process, emergency-power limits, and examples of timelocked exchange-rate and limit changes.

6. **Source and assurance pack**
   - Publish the source and licence inventory, deployed addresses and versions, available audit reports, invariants, build provenance, administrator powers, incident contacts, and monitoring dashboards.
   - Link the applicable [Grassroots Economics repositories](https://github.com/grassrootseconomics).
