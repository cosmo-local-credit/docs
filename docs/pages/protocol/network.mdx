# Protocol network architecture

Several independently governed Pools can connect the Voucher you hold to one you want. Each Pool supplies one step in the exchange, using its own rules and available inventory.

The [CLC software routing reference](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/routing) is the definitive source for route discovery, eligibility, execution, and service interfaces. The software documentation is currently in English. This page explains the participant's journey and responsibilities.

## Production route discovery and execution

In the App's **Credit** view, choose the Voucher you want to **Get** or the one you want to **Spend**. A route might exchange a transport Voucher for a food Voucher through two different Pools.

Finding a connection does not guarantee an exchange. The supported Vouchers, Pool limits, available inventory, fees, Wallet compatibility, and service availability must allow the requested amount.

### Authoritative quote

Review **Route details** before confirming: what you pay, what you receive, every participating Pool, the fees, the minimum received, and when the quote expires. An estimate helps you explore; the quote is the proposal you authorize. Refresh it if it expires or Pool conditions change.

The [routing reference](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/routing) and [service configuration](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/core-api/configuration#credit-router) maintain the technical eligibility rules and quote settings.

### Signed atomic batch

You review and authorize the complete exchange. The current route succeeds as a whole or its Pool swaps revert together if a step fails. This protects against completing only part of the route; it does not guarantee that a later goods or services delivery will happen.

For transaction construction and execution, see [Execute a route](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/routing#execute-a-route).

### Solidity `SwapRouter` and fees

The quote-only Solidity `SwapRouter` and the App's exchange workflow are different software components. Their relationship is explained in [Routing](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/routing).

For participants, the important check is the complete quote. Each Pool may charge fees. A shared route does not merge those Pools or make one Steward responsible for another Pool.

### Receipt and failure states

After submission, check whether the exchange is pending, confirmed, or failed, and keep the receipt. If no route is available, try refreshing, reducing the amount, or choosing another supported input Voucher.

A failed exchange does not change the Issuer's existing promises. A successful exchange records changed balances; fulfillment and discharge still follow the Issuer's terms. See the [practical examples](/introduction/example).

Developers can find transaction and tracking interfaces in the [CLC Core API reference](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/core-api/reference).

## Pool internals

Before relying on a Pool, identify its Steward, contract owner, upgrade authorities, fee recipients, and anyone who can change material settings. A technical controller and an accountable Steward may be different parties.

The software reference maintains [Pool mechanics](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/pools) and [deployment verification](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/deployments). These docs explain [roles and vocabulary](/introduction/concepts), [governance responsibilities](/governance/mechanics), and the [wider CPP design](/white-paper).
