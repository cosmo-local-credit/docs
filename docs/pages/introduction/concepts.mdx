# Concepts and vocabulary

Cosmo-Local Credit connects people who offer goods or services, people who need them, and independently governed Commitment Pools that enable Voucher exchange. This page begins with the terms a participant sees, then explains the technical layers beneath them.

Exact App labels appear in quotation marks where the interface uses a shorter or potentially ambiguous name.

## Everyday terms

| Term | Plain-language meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Voucher** | A transferable commitment—much like a gift card—whose Issuer promises specified goods, services, benefits, or other performance under published terms. |
| **Issuer** | The person or organization that creates the Voucher and remains responsible for what it promises. |
| **Offering** | A catalog record describing goods or services associated with a Voucher. It helps people discover the offer but does not prove availability or fulfillment. |
| **Want** | A published expression of something a participant is looking for. It does not by itself accept an Offering, reserve goods, authorize an exchange, or create an obligation. |
| **Market** | The App's discovery catalog for Vouchers, Pools, Offerings, Wants, and other public information. It is not automatically an exchange venue, seller, endorsement, or guarantee. |
| **Commitment Pool** | A governed arrangement that selects supported Vouchers, publishes exchange rules, holds inventory, and enables exchange. **Pool** is the ordinary shorthand. |
| **Pool Steward** | The accountable person, cooperative, public agency, federation, multisig, service, or other structure that publishes and administers a Pool's rules. |
| **Holder** | A participant controlling a Voucher balance. Holding it does not prove fulfillment, cash value, or a guarantee. |
| **Wallet** | The account and credentials used to authorize token transfers and Pool exchanges. |

“Community member” is informal social language. It does not by itself create admission, ownership, governance, repayment, or other legal rights.

## Discovering and contacting participants

The Market helps people find Vouchers, Offerings, Wants, and Pools. A listing means the App can display a record; it does not prove that its information is accurate, that inventory is available, or that a transaction will succeed.

A participant can publish a Want and can contact another participant through the available contact or messaging features. A contact request, message, booking discussion, or response to a Want does not by itself:

- accept a Voucher or Offering;
- authorize a transfer or Pool swap;
- prove delivery or satisfaction;
- fulfill or discharge a Voucher; or
- make GEF a party to the participants' agreement.

Reviews are public feedback about a Voucher or Pool. They are not the same as private eligibility evidence, operator due diligence, a guarantee, or a finding that fulfillment occurred.

## Vouchers, tokens, and Offerings

A **token** is an on-chain balance or digital asset. Token mechanics can record supply, transfers, expiry, and burning, but they do not by themselves promise goods, services, cash conversion, or redemption.

A **Voucher** is a token or record that an Issuer represents as a redeemable commitment under published terms. Those terms should identify what is promised, who is responsible, where and when it can be presented, material limits, and the fulfillment and complaint process.

An **Offering** is the catalog record for goods or services associated with a Voucher. In ordinary prose, “offer” simply means something a participant proposes; **Offering** is the named App record.

Keep these four layers separate:

1. the token contract and balances;
2. the Voucher designation and published terms;
3. one or more Offering records; and
4. the Issuer's real-world performance.

## Accountable roles and technical control

| Role or authority | Responsibility or power |
| --- | --- |
| **Issuer** | Publishes and fulfills a Voucher commitment. This responsibility does not automatically move merely because contract ownership changes. |
| **Pool Steward** | Publishes and administers Pool rules and remains accountable for any guarantee the Pool expressly advertises. |
| **Pool owner** | The address with current `SwapPool` owner powers, including configuration, fee collection, and available-liquidity withdrawal. |
| **Multisig signers** | Share the technical authority assigned to a multisig Wallet. Changing signers or thresholds does not silently transfer Issuer responsibility, Steward accountability, or legal ownership. |
| **Proxy administrator** | Can upgrade a proxied contract implementation. It may differ from the Pool owner. |
| **Dependency controller** | Controls a registry, limiter, quoter, oracle, fee policy, or protocol-fee controller. |
| **Catalog moderator** | Can correct, hide, retire, or restore catalog records without deleting contracts, balances, or chain history. |
| **Fee recipient** | Receives a configured Pool or protocol fee. It is not necessarily the Pool Steward or owner. |
| **Supporter or liquidity provider** | Contributes assets under separately disclosed terms. The current `SwapPool` creates no automatic Pool shares, repayment, withdrawal, reward, or governance rights. |

## Actions and lifecycle

| App or documentation term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Create or publish a Voucher** | Deploy or configure a token and publish its catalog record and terms. |
| **Issue or mint** | Create token balance under the configured owner or writer authority. |
| **Publish an Offering** | Add a goods or services record beneath a Voucher. |
| **Publish a Want** | Describe a need for discovery and possible contact; it does not itself create a transaction. |
| **Send** | Transfer a token balance to another address. |
| **“Redeem”** | Open the send flow with the token owner's address prefilled. This is redemption presentment; it does not prove fulfillment. |
| **Fulfill** | The Issuer provides the promised goods, services, benefits, or other performance. |
| **Discharge** | Record fulfilled units so they cannot be presented again, for example by receiving, burning, cancelling, or otherwise disabling them under the published terms. |
| **“Request retirement”** | The current owner asks an administrator to retire a Voucher or Pool. The resource remains active while the request is pending, and the request may be approved, rejected, or cancelled. |
| **Retire a Voucher** | After approval, remove the Voucher from active catalog use. Contracts, balances, obligations, and history remain; retirement is not burning or discharge. |
| **Retire a Pool** | After approval, stop new Pool swaps and deposits while allowing available withdrawals. Prior settlement records remain. |
| **Pool deposit or contribution** | Transfer assets into a `SwapPool`. Repayment, withdrawal, reward, or governance rights exist only when separately disclosed. |
| **Pool swap** | Exchange supported assets through one `SwapPool`. This is neither Issuer fulfillment nor automatically a loan. |
| **Route discovery** | Find possible paths through configured Pool relationships. Discovery is an estimate, not an executable promise. |
| **Route quote** | Calculate a time-bounded proposal from Pool state read at one pinned block and simulate it before signing. |
| **Routed exchange** | Submit an ordered set of Pool swaps as one same-chain Wallet batch. In the current flow, every call reverts if any call fails. |
| **Routed-exchange receipt** | Group the tracking status, transaction hash, route hops, settled amounts, and per-hop fees. |
| **Pool liquidity withdrawal** | Let the Pool owner transfer available inventory to a selected address. |
| **Fee collection** | Send accrued Pool fees to the configured recipient. |

Use **swap settlement** for the on-chain transfers and fee accounting that complete a Pool swap. Use **redemption presentment**, **fulfillment**, and **discharge** for the separate real-world Voucher lifecycle.

## Values, credit, limits, and fees

Keep these value terms separate:

- **Offering price:** the amount requested for a listed good or service;
- **Voucher stated value:** a value represented in the Issuer's published terms;
- **Pool exchange rate or quote:** the transaction parameter produced by a Pool's configured quoter;
- **oracle reference:** external data used by a configured valuation module; and
- **redemption terms:** what the Issuer promises and under what conditions.

**Credit** in the App refers to reachable commitments and exchange capacity. It is not automatically personal borrowing, a loan, an Issuer-capacity assessment, or a guarantee.

The label **“Credit limit”** means the Pool's configured token-balance cap: the maximum amount of that token the Pool may hold after a deposit. Proposed rolling, account, and network-wide limits in the White Paper are different mechanisms.

Protocol v1.1.0 can charge a **Pool fee** and an additional **protocol fee** on each Pool swap. A routed exchange itemizes both for every hop. The current App adds no separate routing-service fee. The proposed White Paper model separately describes a future network rake and possible service fees.

## Routes and liquidity

A **route** is an ordered path through one or more independently governed Pools. **Route depth** is the number of Pool swaps in that path.

Not every Voucher, Pool, or Wallet is eligible for routing. Appearing in a Pool does not automatically make a Voucher available through every route. The [software routing reference](https://software.cosmolocal.credit/contracts/routing) maintains the current eligibility rules and configuration in English.

**Available liquidity** is the inventory a Pool can supply under its current rules, limits, and fees. A connection between Pools does not prove that the amount you want is available. Review the final quote; it can expire or become invalid if Pool conditions change.

Current route execution is same-chain and Wallet-initiated. It does not pool governance, automatically rebalance Pool inventory, clear obligations among many parties, or prove fulfillment. Cross-chain HTLC or escrow routing, automatic rebalancing, and batch netting remain proposed or deployment-dependent.

## Protections and evidence

Curation, registry inclusion, a limit, a reserve, App visibility, a Review, or a blockchain transaction does not by itself establish endorsement, Issuer capacity, fulfillment, a guarantee, or insurance.

Any protection should identify the obligated party, covered event, funding, cap, exclusions, duration, evidence, and claim process. On-chain records can show addresses, assets, amounts, timestamps, and contract events. Issuer performance, satisfaction, governance deliberation, and social impact require separate evidence.

## Status words

- **Current:** verified in the CLC App or Protocol v1.1.0 described by these docs.
- **Deployment-dependent:** available only where a particular deployment, Pool, provider, jurisdiction, or published policy enables it.
- **Historical:** evidence or functionality from the earlier Sarafu Network service.
- **Proposed:** a design or roadmap mechanism that is not represented as deployed.
