# Getting Started

Cosmo-Local Credit (CLC) is a progressive web app for creating, discovering, holding, transferring, and exchanging digital assets, including redeemable Vouchers, through independently governed Pools. The public App is available at [cosmolocal.credit](https://cosmolocal.credit).

Grassroots Economics Foundation (GEF) operates the public App and supporting infrastructure. Unless GEF expressly accepts another role for a particular offering or transaction, it is not the Issuer of a user-created Voucher, the Steward of a user-created Pool, a custodian, lender, borrower, guarantor, insurer, or redeemer. Issuers are responsible for their Voucher commitments, and Pool Stewards are responsible for their Pool rules.

Before creating an Account or transacting, read the [Terms of Service](/governance/terms). In particular:

- You must be at least 18 years old, have reached the age of legal majority that applies to you, and have contractual capacity.
- You are responsible for confirming that your Account, Voucher, Pool, or transaction is lawful in every relevant jurisdiction.
- Wallet credentials must be protected. GEF may be unable to recover a lost Wallet or reverse an authorized transaction.
- Public-blockchain transactions and records may be visible, permanent, and irreversible.
- A listing in the App is not a GEF endorsement or a guarantee of redemption, value, liquidity, convertibility, or cash-out.

## Start with the live App

### 1. Browse the public market

Open [cosmolocal.credit](https://cosmolocal.credit) to explore public marketplace listings before signing in. Review the responsible Issuer, the promised goods or services, location, availability, and other published information. Visibility in the market does not mean that GEF has guaranteed or independently valued an offering.

### 2. Create an Account and Wallet

Follow the App's onboarding flow to create an Account and a supported Wallet, or to connect or import a Wallet when that option is available. Provide accurate profile information and carefully store every passkey, paper-wallet credential, private key, password, or recovery method associated with the Wallet.

Do not share signing credentials. Before approving any Wallet request, confirm the assets, amounts, recipient, Pool, fees, and other displayed transaction details.

### 3. Inspect the Voucher and Pool

Before accepting or acquiring a Voucher, inspect its terms. These should identify the Issuer and explain what the Voucher can be redeemed for, its supply or capacity, unit and valuation, expiry, redemption process, geography, timing, fees, restrictions, and available remedies.

Before using a Pool, inspect the Pool Steward and the published rules: accepted assets, valuations, fees, limits, inventory, governance and upgrade powers, pause controls, and any reserve or guarantee. A Pool's displayed value is not itself a promise that an Issuer will redeem a Voucher or that another asset will always be available.

### 4. Review and execute a Swap

Where a Pool supports a pair, the App can request a quote and prepare a Swap. Quotes can be temporary, and a transaction can fail or change because of inventory, limits, fees, network conditions, authorization, or Pool configuration. Review the final parameters immediately before confirming.

An ordinary Swap exchanges assets under the displayed transaction parameters and Pool rules. The direction of an exchange—including exchanging a Voucher for a stablecoin or another asset—does not by itself make the transaction a loan or repayment.

### 5. Redeem or continue using the asset

To redeem a Voucher, follow the Issuer's published process. The Issuer, rather than GEF or an unrelated Pool Steward, is responsible for providing the promised goods, services, benefits, or other performance. A completed redemption should be recorded in the manner specified by the Issuer and applicable system so that the same claim cannot be used twice.

### 6. Create a Voucher or Pool

Users with the necessary authority and legal capacity can use supported App flows to create Vouchers and Pools.

- A **Voucher Issuer** must publish complete, accurate terms, issue within its capacity, keep material information current, and honor the redemption commitment it makes.
- A **Pool Steward** must publish and consistently apply the Pool's admission, curation, valuation, fee, limit, inventory, governance, conflict, pause, and risk rules. Any guarantee, reserve, or insurance arrangement must state who provides it and its exact limits.

Creating a smart contract or listing does not make an activity lawful. Issuers and Stewards remain responsible for required registrations, disclosures, permissions, taxes, consumer protections, and other obligations.

### 7. Install the PWA, if useful

On supported browsers and devices, the App can be installed as a progressive web app. Installation is optional and does not change the Account, Wallet, transaction risks, or applicable terms.

## What is available and what is optional

The public App provides a market and supported flows for Accounts, Wallets, Vouchers, Pools, transfers, and Pool Swaps. Particular features depend on the active deployment, asset, Pool configuration, device, network, and user eligibility.

The wider CLC protocol can also support registries, routing services, oracle-based pricing, limiters, fee policies, monitoring, liquidity programs, and other governance arrangements. Multi-hop routing, cross-Pool netting, shared insurance, token-based governance, and fiat or stablecoin on/off-ramps are optional or future capabilities unless a specific deployment clearly says they are active. Their discussion in these docs or the White Paper is not a promise that GEF currently provides them.

## When a Swap is expressly a loan

A Swap is subject to loan mechanics only when a Pool expressly presents it before confirmation as a repayable advance. A designated Swap Loan needs its own Transaction Terms, including the parties or roles, advance and repayment amounts, due date, interest or fees, collateral rights, assignment and Holder changes, partial settlement, any redemption at an agreed Full Value, evidence of discharge, default rules, and lawful remedies.

There is no CLC-wide assumption of a one-year term, zero interest, or any other missing credit term. Applicable law and the expressly disclosed Transaction Terms—not asset direction or informal labels—determine the arrangement's legal effect.
