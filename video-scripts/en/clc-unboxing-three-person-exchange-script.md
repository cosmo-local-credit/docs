# Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing: three people exchange what they offer

- **Target length:** 4 minutes 15 seconds to 4 minutes 50 seconds
- **Audience:** People seeing Cosmo-Local Credit for the first time
- **Format:** Edited desktop screen capture using three separate App accounts
- **Outcome:** The viewer sees how three Vouchers can move through one Pool so that a farmer receives dental care, a mechanic receives food, and a dentist receives vehicle repair.

## Ready-to-paste video description

What happens when a farmer needs dental care, a mechanic needs food, and a dentist needs his car repaired?

In this **Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing**, each person creates a Voucher for something they can provide: food baskets, vehicle repair, or dental services. A Voucher works much like a digital gift card from its creator. The dentist then creates **Health Market**, a curated Pool where these Vouchers can be exchanged under clear rules.

The farmer exchanges Food Basket Vouchers for Dental Services Vouchers and uses them for care. The mechanic exchanges Vehicle Repair Vouchers for the Food Basket Vouchers and uses them for a delivery. Finally, the dentist withdraws the Vehicle Repair Vouchers from the Pool and uses them to have his car fixed.

Each Voucher has an identifiable issuer and published terms. The Pool shows which Vouchers it accepts, how they are valued, and how much it can hold. Everyone receives something useful through what they can offer, without needing cash for the exchanges shown.

Explore Cosmo-Local Credit at `https://cosmolocal.credit`.

Built and maintained by Grassroots Economics Foundation: `https://grassrootseconomics.org`

## The exchange at a glance

| Person | Offers | Needs | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Farmer | Food baskets | Dental care | Swaps Food Basket Vouchers for Dental Services Vouchers |
| Mechanic | Vehicle repair | Food | Swaps Vehicle Repair Vouchers for Food Basket Vouchers |
| Dentist | Dental care | Vehicle repair | Seeds Health Market with Dental Services Vouchers, then withdraws Vehicle Repair Vouchers |

The Pool moves the Vouchers in a circle:

`FOOD → DENTAL` · `REPAIR → FOOD` · `DENTAL → REPAIR`

## Terms to explain once

- A **Voucher** works much like a gift card from the person who provides the good or service.
- An **Offering** explains the specific good or service available and how the Voucher can be used.
- A **Pool** is a curated place where approved Vouchers can be exchanged under published rules.
- A Pool **credit limit** is the maximum value of a Voucher that the Pool can hold. It is not a personal loan.
- **Redeem** sends a Voucher back to its issuer. The issuer then provides the good or service according to the Voucher's terms.

After each definition, prefer the shorter words Voucher, offer, Pool, limit, exchange, and use.

## Demonstration setup

Use three prepared, fictional accounts:

- **FARMER:** creates Food Basket Voucher and a Food basket Offering; needs dental care.
- **MECHANIC:** creates Vehicle Repair Voucher and a Vehicle repair Offering; needs food.
- **DENTIST:** creates Dental Services Voucher and a Dental treatment Offering; needs vehicle repair; owns Health Market.

Use the actual quantities, prices, rates, fees, and symbols visible in the recorded demonstration. The important preparation is:

1. Each Voucher has clear terms, a safe demonstration contact method, and an Offering.
2. The dentist creates Health Market and approves all three Vouchers.
3. The dentist deposits enough Dental Services Vouchers for the farmer's swap.
4. Food Basket Voucher and Vehicle Repair Voucher each have enough available Pool credit for the planned swaps.
5. The swap amounts leave enough Food Basket Vouchers for the mechanic and enough Vehicle Repair Vouchers for the dentist.

### Scene 3 — The dentist creates Health Market

**Time:** 1:05–1:38

**Visuals:** Highlight the dentist. Show **Create pool**, the name **Health Market**, its description, unit of account, pricing method, and published Pool page.

**Voiceover:**

> Next, the dentist creates a Pool called Health Market. A Pool is a curated place where selected Vouchers can be exchanged under clear rules.
>
> The dentist is the Pool owner and Steward. He chooses which Vouchers Health Market accepts, how the Pool values them, and how much of each it can hold.

**On-screen text:** `Health Market` · `A curated place to exchange approved Vouchers`

### Scene 4 — The dentist prepares the Pool

**Time:** 1:38–2:15

**Visuals:** Show **Approve voucher** for Dental Services Voucher, then **Deposit**. Show **Approve voucher** for Food Basket Voucher and Vehicle Repair Voucher. Pause on **Exchange rate** and **Credit limit** for each.

**Voiceover:**

> First, the dentist approves his Dental Services Voucher and deposits some into Health Market. These are the Vouchers the farmer will be able to receive.
>
> He then reviews and approves the farmer's Food Basket Voucher and the mechanic's Vehicle Repair Voucher. For each one, he sets an exchange rate and a credit limit.
>
> The credit limit says how much of that Voucher the Pool can accept. It is a limit for the Pool, not a personal loan to the farmer or mechanic.

**On-screen callouts:** `Approve voucher` · `Deposit` · `Exchange rate` · `Credit limit`

### Scene 5 — The farmer exchanges food for dental care

**Time:** 2:15–2:55

**Visuals:** Open Health Market in the farmer's account. Show available credit if it appears in the recording. Select **Swap**, place Dental Services Voucher under **You receive** and Food Basket Voucher under **You send**, review the quote and fees, and select **Confirm swap**. Then show the dental visit and the farmer's **Redeem** flow.

**Voiceover:**

> The farmer opens Health Market and starts a swap. Under You send, she chooses Food Basket Vouchers. Under You receive, she chooses Dental Services Vouchers.
>
> She checks the rate, fees, and final amounts, then confirms. Her Food Basket Vouchers enter the Pool, and the Dental Services Vouchers enter her Wallet.
>
> She arranges her visit with the dentist. After receiving care, she selects Redeem and sends the Dental Services Vouchers back to the dentist who issued them.

**On-screen text:** `You send: FOOD` · `You receive: DENTAL` · `Redeem with the issuer`

### Scene 6 — The mechanic exchanges repairs for food

**Time:** 2:55–3:35

**Visuals:** Open Health Market in the mechanic's account. Select **Swap**, place Food Basket Voucher under **You receive** and Vehicle Repair Voucher under **You send**, review the quote, and select **Confirm swap**. Then show **Redeem** and the food delivery.

**Voiceover:**

> The Food Basket Vouchers are now waiting in Health Market. The mechanic needs food and can offer vehicle repair.
>
> He sends Vehicle Repair Vouchers into the Pool and receives Food Basket Vouchers. After checking the exchange details, he confirms the swap.
>
> The mechanic then redeems the Food Basket Vouchers with the farmer and receives his food delivery.

**On-screen text:** `You send: REPAIR` · `You receive: FOOD` · `Food delivered by the issuer`

**Time:** 3:35–4:10

**Visuals:** In the dentist's Health Market controls, open **Withdraw liquidity**. Select Vehicle Repair Voucher, enter the amount and recipient, confirm the withdrawal, and show the Vouchers in the dentist's Wallet. Then show **Redeem** and the repaired car.

**Voiceover:**

## Product-accuracy and safety notes

- This script was checked against CLC App `origin/develop` commit `704480cf2173dd9d95f3feadda3748999df2b072`. Recheck labels, permissions, rates, fees, balances, and limits against the filming build before final editing.
- Use fictional accounts, Wallets, contact details, locations, dental information, and vehicle details. Do not show passkeys, recovery information, private keys, real phone numbers, or precise home locations.
- A Pool **credit limit** caps how much value of a Voucher the Pool may hold. It is Pool exchange capacity, not cash, a personal credit line, a debt, or a promise that a swap will always be available.
- Approval alone adds no inventory. Health Market must hold enough Dental Services Vouchers before the farmer can receive them.
- The farmer's swap moves Food Basket Vouchers into Health Market and Dental Services Vouchers out. The mechanic's swap moves Vehicle Repair Vouchers in and Food Basket Vouchers out.
- Let the App show its live rate and fees. Use the final quoted amounts from the recording rather than implying a one-for-one exchange.
- **Withdraw liquidity** is available to the Pool's on-chain owner. It moves available Pool inventory to the chosen recipient. Show the dentist withdrawing only the Vehicle Repair Vouchers used in this story.
- The owner-withdrawal ability should not be hidden in the edit. It is an important part of understanding who controls Health Market.
- **Redeem** sends Vouchers to their verified issuer. The button records a transfer; it does not itself prove that food, dental care, or repair was provided.
- Show or state that the farmer, dentist, and mechanic provide their goods and services directly under their own Voucher terms. The CLC App and the Pool do not provide, inspect, insure, or guarantee that work.
- “Accountability” in this video means identifiable issuers, published Voucher terms, visible Pool rules, and recorded exchanges. It does not mean that the App guarantees performance.
- “Without needing cash” refers to the three exchanges shown. Participants may still have other costs, taxes, fees, or needs outside this example.
- Keep every spoken sentence short and self-contained so the script can be translated later.
