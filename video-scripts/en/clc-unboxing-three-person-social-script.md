# Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing: short social video

- **Target length:** 1 minute 35 seconds to 1 minute 55 seconds
- **Audience:** People encountering Cosmo-Local Credit for the first time
- **Format:** A short edit of the three-account desktop screen capture
- **Tone:** Friendly, practical, and easy to understand
- **Primary call to action:** Explore `cosmolocal.credit`

## YouTube description

A farmer needs dental care, a mechanic needs food, and a dentist needs vehicle repair. Each creates a Voucher for something useful they can provide. The dentist connects the Vouchers through a Pool called **Health Market**.

The farmer exchanges Food Basket Vouchers for dental care. The mechanic exchanges Vehicle Repair Vouchers for food. The dentist then uses the repair Vouchers for his car. This short Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing shows how clear promises and Pool rules can connect what people offer with what they need.

## Suggested social caption

What happens when a farmer needs dental care, a mechanic needs food, and a dentist needs his car repaired?

In this Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing, three people create Vouchers for what they can provide. A dentist connects those Vouchers in a Pool called **Health Market**. The farmer exchanges Food Basket Vouchers for dental care. The mechanic exchanges Repair Vouchers for food. The dentist then uses the Repair Vouchers to have his car fixed.

Each Voucher has a clear issuer and terms. The Pool shows which Vouchers it accepts, how they are valued, and how much it can hold. Everyone receives something useful through what they can offer, without needing cash for these exchanges.

## Creative direction

Introduce only the ideas needed for the story:

- A **Voucher** works much like a gift card from the person who provides the good or service.
- An **Offering** explains what the Voucher can be used for.
- A **Pool** is a curated place where approved Vouchers can be exchanged under clear rules.
- A Pool **credit limit** is how much of a Voucher the Pool can accept. It is not a personal loan.

Use short chapter cards: **OFFER**, **CONNECT**, **EXCHANGE**, **USE**. Highlight only the account acting in each shot. Avoid coins, loans, trading charts, and unexplained financial language.

### Scene 2 — Each person creates a Voucher

**Time:** 0:16–0:35

**Visuals:** Cut between **Food Basket Voucher**, **Vehicle Repair Voucher**, and **Dental Services Voucher**, then show an Offering beneath each one.

**Voiceover:**

### Scene 3 — The dentist creates Health Market

**Time:** 0:35–0:57

**Visuals:** Show **Create pool**, then **Health Market**. Show **Approve voucher** for all three Vouchers, **Deposit** for Dental Services Voucher, and the Pool **Credit limit** fields for the other two.

**Voiceover:**

> The dentist creates a Pool called Health Market. Think of it as a small, curated exchange. He deposits Dental Services Vouchers, then approves the farmer's and mechanic's Vouchers and sets how much of each the Pool can accept.

**On-screen text:** `Health Market` · `Approved Vouchers and clear limits`

### Scene 4 — The farmer and mechanic exchange

**Time:** 0:57–1:24

**Visuals:** Show the farmer's swap from food to dental care, then her **Redeem** flow. Show the mechanic's swap from vehicle repair to food, then his **Redeem** flow.

**Voiceover:**

> The farmer sends Food Basket Vouchers into the Pool and receives Dental Services Vouchers. She uses them with the dentist for her care. Her food Vouchers remain in the Pool.
>
> The mechanic then sends Vehicle Repair Vouchers into the Pool and receives the Food Basket Vouchers. He uses them with the farmer for a food delivery.

**On-screen text:** `FOOD → DENTAL` · `REPAIR → FOOD`

## Accuracy and editing notes

- Use fictional accounts, contact details, Wallets, locations, and health information.
- Show the live values, rates, fees, and credit limits from the recorded demonstration. Do not imply that all Vouchers are equal in value.
- The dentist gives the farmer's and mechanic's **Vouchers** Pool credit limits. He does not give either person a cash loan or personal line of credit.
- The Pool must hold enough Dental Services Vouchers before the farmer's swap. Approval alone does not add Vouchers to the Pool.
- The farmer's swap moves Food Basket Vouchers into the Pool and Dental Services Vouchers out. The mechanic's swap then moves Vehicle Repair Vouchers in and Food Basket Vouchers out.
- **Withdraw liquidity** is an owner action. It moves available Vehicle Repair Vouchers from Health Market to the dentist's Wallet.
- **Redeem** sends a Voucher back to its verified issuer. The actual dental care, food delivery, and car repair take place directly between the people involved.
- “Without needing cash” describes the three exchanges shown. It is not a promise that every participant, service, fee, or Pool will operate without cash.
