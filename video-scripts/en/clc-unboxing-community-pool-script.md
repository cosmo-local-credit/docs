# Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing: a community-owned Pool

- **Target length:** 3 minutes 30 seconds to 4 minutes 30 seconds
- **Audience:** People who have seen the three-person Unboxing or already understand Vouchers and Pools
- **Format:** Edited desktop screen capture using the dentist, farmer, and mechanic demonstration accounts
- **Outcome:** Viewers see how one person can transfer Pool control to a multisig, how two-of-three approval works, and what each participant can still do individually.

## YouTube description

What changes when a Commitment Pool is controlled by a community instead of one person?

In this Cosmo-Local Credit Unboxing, the dentist transfers control of **Health Market** to a multisig wallet shared by the farmer, mechanic, and dentist. Any two of the three signers must approve contract-level Pool changes, including approving a Voucher or changing its credit limit.

Each participant can still deposit Vouchers into the Pool from their own Wallet. Depositing does not require two signatures. The Pool's credit limits apply to the Vouchers it can hold—not to personal borrowing.

Shared technical control gives the three signers a clear way to approve important Pool changes together. Each Voucher issuer remains responsible for the goods or services promised in that Voucher's terms.

Explore Cosmo-Local Credit at `cosmolocal.credit`.

## Starting point

This video follows the original three-person Unboxing:

- The **farmer** offers Food Basket Vouchers and needs dental care.
- The **mechanic** offers Vehicle Repair Vouchers and needs food.
- The **dentist** offers Dental Services Vouchers and created **Health Market**.

Before filming, prepare a supported multisig wallet with all three demonstration Wallet addresses as signers and an approval threshold of **2 of 3**. Use fictional accounts, contact details, Wallets, locations, and health information.

## Terms to explain once

- A **multisig wallet** shares technical control among several signers.
- **2 of 3 approvals required** means any two of the three signers must approve a queued contract transaction before it can execute.
- A Pool **credit limit** is the maximum value of one Voucher that the Pool can hold. It is not credit given to a person and is not a personal loan.
- A **deposit** sends Vouchers from one participant's Wallet into the Pool. It is the participant's transaction, so it does not require Pool-owner quorum.

### Scene 3 — Transfer Health Market to the multisig

**Time:** 0:58–1:30

**Visuals:** In Health Market, open the owner controls and select **Transfer ownership**. Enter the multisig address. Pause on the notice explaining that the new owner receives Pool-management and liquidity-withdrawal authority. Confirm the transfer. Return to the Pool and show **Multisig owned · 2 of 3 signers**.

**Voiceover:**

> The dentist opens Health Market and selects Transfer ownership. He transfers the Pool contract to the multisig address. When the transaction confirms, the multisig gains the Pool's contract-management and liquidity-withdrawal authority.

**On-screen callouts:** `Transfer ownership` · `Multisig owned · 2 of 3 signers`

### Scene 4 — Approve a Voucher together

**Time:** 1:30–2:15

**Visuals:** Have one signer open Health Market and select **Approve voucher** for a prepared demonstration Voucher. Enter its **Exchange rate** and **Credit limit**, then propose the action. **Voiceover:**

> A signer can now propose a contract-level Pool change. Here, the farmer proposes approving a Voucher and setting its exchange rate and credit limit.
>
> The proposal waits in Multisig transactions. The mechanic reviews it and provides the second approval. With two of three approvals, the transaction can execute and the Voucher becomes approved in Health Market.
>
> The credit limit controls how much of that Voucher the Pool may hold. It is not a loan or a personal spending limit.

**On-screen callouts:** `Approve voucher` · `Credit limit` · `Multisig transactions` · `2 of 3 approvals`

**Time:** 2:15–2:43

**Voiceover:**

## Accuracy and production notes

- Recheck the filming build against CLC App `origin/develop` commit `704480cf2173dd9d95f3feadda3748999df2b072` or the later commit actually filmed.
- Use the App's visible labels: **Transfer ownership**, **Signers and approvals**, **Approvals required**, **Multisig transactions**, **Approve voucher**, **Manage voucher**, **Credit limit**, and **Deposit**.
- The transfer changes the owner address of the Pool and its connected owner-controlled contracts. Do not describe it as transferring the legal ownership of participants' businesses, Vouchers, or obligations.
- A two-of-three threshold governs queued Safe transactions. It does not mean that every catalog edit, message, deposit, or swap needs two approvals.
- Deposits use the participant's own Wallet authority. The Pool must already approve the Voucher, and its remaining capacity must allow the deposit.
- A credit limit is attached to an approved Voucher in one Pool. Never show or describe it as USD 200 lent to a person.
- Multisig signers may have separately authorized backend catalog-management powers. State those powers separately from contract ownership and quorum.
- Each Voucher issuer remains responsible for the real-world goods or services described in that Voucher's terms.
- Show live transaction details and fees from the filming build. Never expose private keys, passkeys, card secrets, recovery information, or real personal data.
